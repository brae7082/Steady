Exercise progressions

Choose a variation, adjust its target if needed, then tap Save. Changes will be used in your next workout. The app can also advance you automatically after successful sets.

How progression works

Strength repetitions progress from 3 × 5 to 3 × 8. Core repetitions progress from 3 × 8 to 3 × 12. Holds build toward 3 × 30 seconds (dip support: 3 × 60 seconds). Negative exercises count repetitions, with the goal of gradually slowing each descent toward 10 seconds. Unilateral movements are performed on both sides.

Reminder time

The time is saved, but scheduled phone notifications are not yet implemented. Reliable background delivery requires HTTPS hosting and a push service.

Install on Android

After publishing the app to an HTTPS website, open that address in Chrome on your Android phone. Tap the three-dot menu → Install app or Add to Home screen. Opening a local ZIP or file will not offer proper PWA installation.

Equipment

Resistance bands, dumbbells, pull-up bar, sturdy table, sturdy chairs, and a dedicated row station.

Streaks

Your streak counts consecutive calendar weeks with all three sessions completed. Rest days and missed sessions won't erase your lifetime total.
